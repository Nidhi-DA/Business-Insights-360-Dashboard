## 📊 Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to track, analyze, and optimize business performance across multiple departments. The report provides a 360-degree view of corporate health by integrating data from finance, sales, marketing, and supply chain domains.

## 🔑 Key Features & Dashboards
The _[Power BI file](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/Business_insights_360_pdf.pdf.)_  includes six main views:
1. [Home](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI-%20Home%20Page.png.) : Navigation hub providing clean links to all report pages.
2. [Finance View](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI%20-%20Finance%20Page.png.) : Complete Profit & Loss (P&L) statement tracking Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit trends over time.
3. [Sales View](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI%20-%20Sales%20Page.png.) : Detailed analysis of Customer Performance and Product Performance combined with Gross Margin matrix metrics.
4. [Marketing View](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI-Marketing%20Page.png.) : Segment-level and regional analysis tracking Unit Economics, Net Profit matrixes, and product performance.
5. [Supply Chain View](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI-%20Supply%20%20Chain%20Page.png.) : Operations-focused tracking of Forecast Accuracy, Net Errors, Absolute Errors, and Out of Stock (OOS) risk profiles.
6. [Executive View](https://github.com/Nidhi-DA/Business-Insights-360-Dashboard/blob/main/BI%20-%20Executive%20Page.png.) : A high-level consolidated view built for C-suite decisions, highlighting top customers, market trends, and revenue distribution.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project
The primary objective of this project was to dissolve data silos across multiple operational departments and unify them into a single **360-degree enterprise tracking tool**. Large organizations often struggle with fragmented reporting where finance, sales, and supply chain teams operate on disconnected data sources. This project provides a "single source of truth," allowing executives to track real-time operational efficiency, evaluate promotional impacts, monitor financial leakage, and make quick, data-driven decisions.

## 💎 Business Importance & Impact
Deploying an enterprise-grade report like Business Insights 360 drives substantial business value by optimizing cost structures and highlighting hidden revenue opportunities:
* **Financial Oversight & Margin Protection**: By breaking down Gross Margin % and Net Profit % at the country, segment, and product level, the dashboard instantly exposes regions where high sales volume fails to translate into profitability due to rising operational costs.
* **Supply Chain Optimization**: Tracking Forecast Accuracy and Out-of-Stock (OOS) risk profiles bridges the gap between sales demand and inventory management. This directly minimizes warehousing overhead costs and prevents lost sales opportunities from stock shortages.
* **Marketing ROI Acceleration**: Segment-level unit economic analysis helps marketing teams dynamically allocate their ad spend away from low-performing channels and toward high-margin customer cohorts.
* **C-Suite Strategic Alignment**: The Executive View consolidates multi-departmental data into a concise macro summary, giving leadership immediate clarity on operational blindspots without requiring them to drill down into multiple isolated files.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Power BI Desktop & Service**: Deployed data models, built complex DAX measures, configured row-level security (RLS), and managed workspace deployments on Power BI Server.
* **Advanced DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Authored complex measures, calculated columns, and time-intelligence functions to track dynamic metrics like YTD Sales, Gross Margin %, and Forecast Accuracy.
* **Power Query & M Code**: Executed comprehensive ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes, including data profiling, schema validation, handling null values, and custom column transformations.
* **Data Modeling & Star Schema**: Designed an optimized analytical data model utilizing a Star Schema structure with clearly defined Fact and Dimension tables to ensure high-performance report filtering.
* **Data Visualization & UX/UI Design**: Formatted dashboards with clean navigation bars, page tooltips, conditional formatting, and intuitive layouts structured for C-suite decision-making.
* **SQL & Data Warehousing**: Queried, filtered, and aggregated relational database tables to prep initial data sources and validate Power BI report totals against backend database metrics.
